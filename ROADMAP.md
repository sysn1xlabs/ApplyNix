# Roadmap

## v0.1 — shipped
Local profile, text ingestion, vocabulary analysis, extractive tailoring, provenance, HTML/Markdown exports, batch preparation, review dashboard and SQLite tracking.

## v0.2 — richer preparation
Structured requirement parser with negation and certification handling; evidence levels; project rendering; validated résumé import; DOCX and PDF exports; configurable role and location preferences; follow-up reminders and interview preparation.

## v0.3 — optional AI
Provider interface for hosted and local models; explicit data-sharing consent; structured outputs; source-grounded rewrite proposals; user review of every new wording. Evidence references alone must not be treated as proof of semantic support.

## v0.4 — supported job ingestion
URL extraction with public-address validation, redirect revalidation, download limits and timeouts; source-specific job feeds; duplicate job detection; discovery filters and ranking.

## v0.5 — assisted application adapters
Adapter capability declarations, field mapping, unknown-answer handoff, human verification handoff, per-draft approval bound to the final payload, receipt verification and recoverable failures. Start with manual final submission before supporting confirmed submission where appropriate.

## Later
Selected-batch approval, encrypted local data, OS secret storage, analytics and desktop packaging. No blind bulk submissions or CAPTCHA circumvention.
